Risk Assessment Framework

SpendSense evaluates every purchase against your disposable income using these thresholds:

LOW RISK
≤ 30%

Purchase uses a small portion of disposable income. Generally safe to proceed.

MEDIUM RISK
30 - 60%

Significant portion of disposable income. Consider alternatives or timing.

HIGH RISK
> 60%

Majority of disposable income. Evaluate carefully and explore options.

HARD STOP
N/A

Expenses exceed income or purchase exceeds disposable. Cannot recommend.

Budgeting Strategies

50/30/20 Rule

50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings. A simple framework for balanced spending.

24-Hour Rule

Wait 24 hours before any non-essential purchase over 5% of your income.

Zero-Based Budgeting

Assign every shilling a job. Income minus all allocations should equal zero.

Emergency Fund First

Build 3-6 months of expenses before discretionary spending.

How Confidence Scoring Works

Disposable Ratio

Primary factor: what percentage of your disposable income the purchase represents.

Savings Buffer

Higher confidence when savings goals are preserved after the purchase.
